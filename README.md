# TaskFlow Business Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

TaskFlow is a fictional subscription-based productivity startup that offers Basic, Pro, and Business subscription plans with monthly and annual billing options.

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze customer acquisition, trial conversion, subscription activity, cancellations, and revenue. The dashboard helps stakeholders understand business performance and identify areas that need improvement.

**Central Business Question:** Is TaskFlow growing in a healthy way, and where should the business focus its attention?

## Business Objectives

- Identify the acquisition channels generating the highest customer volume.
- Compare conversion status across acquisition channels.
- Analyze active and cancelled customers across subscription plans and billing cycles.
- Identify the plan and billing combinations generating the highest revenue.
- Provide actionable recommendations to support business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query for data preparation
- Excel for source data
- Data visualization and exploratory analysis
- Business intelligence and KPI reporting

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Customer Acquisition Analysis
Compares customer volume across Paid Social, Organic Search, Direct, Referral, and Partner channels.

### 2. Conversion Analysis
Compares converted and non-converted customer shares across acquisition channels for users who started a trial.

### 3. Customer Status Analysis
Examines active and cancelled customer shares across plan and billing combinations, including Basic, Pro, and Business plans.

### 4. Revenue Analysis
Compares revenue generated by monthly and annual subscription combinations.

### 5. Interactive Filters
Includes country selection and a month filter to explore the dashboard from different perspectives.

## Key Business Insights

1. **Paid Social leads customer acquisition:** It brings the highest customer volume among the displayed channels, with 537 customers.
2. **Conversion performance varies by channel:** The dashboard shows different converted and non-converted customer shares across acquisition channels.
3. **Basic Monthly has the highest cancellation share:** Among the displayed plan and billing combinations, Basic Monthly has the largest cancelled share.
4. **Business Annual leads revenue:** It generates the highest displayed revenue, approximately 2.6M.

## Business Recommendations

- Review Paid Social targeting and campaign quality to improve the proportion of customers who convert.
- Evaluate Partner and Referral acquisition strategies using conversion performance and acquisition cost data before increasing investment.
- Investigate why Basic Monthly customers cancel more frequently and explore retention improvements.
- Study the performance of the Business Annual plan to understand opportunities for sustainable revenue growth.

## Data Preparation

The project requirements identify the following data quality issues for review:

- Extra spaces and inconsistent naming in acquisition channels.
- Inconsistent subscription plan and billing cycle labels.
- Inconsistent trial conversion and subscription status values.
- Extra spaces in country names.
- Duplicate Customer IDs associated with different names or details.

Duplicate Customer IDs require business validation before records are removed or combined. The appropriate treatment depends on whether they represent genuine duplicate records or distinct customer records.
